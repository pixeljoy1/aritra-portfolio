## At a glance
- **What I did:** drew plans, modelled a three-storey house with a roof terrace in SketchUp, textured it, and rendered exteriors, interiors and a walkthrough video.


## Process
I started with floor plans and elevations, then built the house in SketchUp as a three-storey volume with a roof terrace. Materials came next: wood, concrete and stone textures. Finally I rendered exteriors, interiors and a walkthrough video.

## Reflection
This was my first time carrying one space from a flat plan to something I could walk through. It taught me to plan the rooms on paper before touching the model.
