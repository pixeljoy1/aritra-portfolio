## Process
**S3:** a photo study of natural and artificial, interior and exterior light: front lit, side lit, backlit, falloff, LED, signage, stadium lights. Natural light needs patience; artificial light is controllable; direction changes mood.

**S5:** Unreal Engine high-resolution screenshots (September 2026).

## Reflection
Photographing real light first made the engine work easier. I already knew what I was trying to copy: where the light comes from, how fast it falls off, and what colour the shadows are. Three things I took from it: natural light rewards patience, artificial light is controllable, and the direction of the light changes the mood more than anything else.
