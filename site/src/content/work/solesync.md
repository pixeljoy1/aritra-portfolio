## At a glance
- **Problem:** buying and caring for sneakers in India has a trust gap: "Is it real? Is it fair? Will I ruin it?"
- **What I did:** a fair-price verified shop, on-call at-home care and a "Sneaker Passport" (authenticity, price, care history); research, a 17-section Markdown design brief, and 3 prompted prototype iterations, each critiqued.
- **What happened:** 4 of 5 research insights fully met in the prototype, 1 partly. No real users, sales or launch.

## Process
Stage 1: desk research, ecosystem audit, a survey (n = 120) and 8 interviews, reduced to 5 patterns, 5 insights and 4 design principles (Prove it; Price with logic; Teach, then step back; Care is ownership). Stage 2: brief, prompt, critique, revise.
