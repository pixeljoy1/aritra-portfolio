## At a glance
- **Problem:** make a place that tells its story without a single line of text.
- **What I did:** built one abandoned-looking building on a rocky base, with a rusted satellite dish, crates, barrels and a wire cage, so every surface hints at what happened here.
- **What happened:** a finished, textured environment shown in final renders and in the full deck below.

## Process
**Concept and narrative.** I began with a board of references and texture swatches: moss, rust, stained concrete. Those decided the mood before I modelled anything.

**Initial blocking.** I built the building in plain grey first, so I could judge proportion and silhouette without colour helping.

**Final modelling.** I added the details that carry the story: the dish on the roof, the lamp post, the small hut, crates and barrels.

**Texturing.** Layered dirt, dust and grunge maps gave the walls moss and weathering. The hut got a stop sign, and the cage got rust and chipped paint.

**Final renders.** Lit and rendered in Marmoset Toolbag, from wide views down to close-ups of the cage and the roof.

## Reflection
Blocking in grey first saved the most time. If the silhouette worked in grey, the texturing only had to add character, not rescue the shape.
