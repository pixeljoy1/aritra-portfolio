## At a glance
- **What I did:** built a forest clearing with a whirlpool in Maya, a cratered moon asset, and fog particles, planned with a moodboard and storyboard.
- **Process:** moodboard, storyboard, SketchUp layout, Maya build, engine check.

## Process
**Moodboard and storyboard.** I collected dark, misty, lamp-lit scenes to set the feeling, then storyboarded the sequence so the space had a beginning and an end.

**Layout.** A quick SketchUp plan placed the clearing, the trees and the whirlpool at the centre.

**Build.** In Maya I modelled and placed the forest, textured a cratered moon, and animated the whirlpool with fog particles drifting over it.

**Check in the engine.** I looked at the scene in real time to see if the mood from the moodboard had survived.

## Reflection
The moodboard did the most work. Every later choice, from the fog to the colour of the water, could be tested against it.
