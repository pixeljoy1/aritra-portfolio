## At a glance
- **Problem:** I wanted people to feel, in under a minute, what a tiger is up against on a road full of human-made danger.
- **What I did:** turned my own Grade 10 illustration into a mobile-first endless runner with four loops that pull against each other: survival, hunting, distance and conservation.
- **What happened:** it is playable now on the [play page](../../play/), where the full making-of is laid out step by step.

## Process
I started from the drawing, not from a game idea. The illustration already had the world: a tiger, a road, traps, boar and poachers. The job was to protect that look while giving it rules.

The HUD became part of the brand: yellow pill shapes with red lettering, kept exactly as I drew them. The road is built in one-point perspective with three lanes so it can be played with one thumb on a phone.

I kept the scope small on purpose. There is one road, no levels and no multiplayer. That made it possible to finish, play and judge the core loop first.

## Reflection
The biggest lesson was that a drawing is a promise about feel. Every rule I added had to keep that promise, so the tiger's stamina, the cubs and the habitat tiers all exist to say something about conservation, not just to score points.
